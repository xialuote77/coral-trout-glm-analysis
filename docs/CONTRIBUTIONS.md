# Contributions

Individual coursework by Kunze (Charlotte) Lyu: exploratory analysis, count-GLM formulation, Poisson and quasi-Poisson fitting, interaction tests, dispersion assessment, residual/leverage/influence diagnostics, sensitivity refits, confidence intervals and the consulting-style report.

Portfolio curation extracts the completed answer section from the QMD, supplies a standalone R runner with relative paths, seeds display jitter, adds machine-readable output exports and consistency checks, adds a fitted-count interval chart, and writes bilingual documentation. These additions are distinguished from the original coursework. Original model choices, coefficients, inference and report analysis are retained.

The source data and teaching preprocessing were supplied by the course and derive from AIMS. The author did not independently collect these field observations or design the zoning scheme. Assignment instructions, grading text and student identifiers are excluded from the public copy. No new licence is assigned to supplied course data or upstream materials.
